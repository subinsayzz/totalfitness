ABOUT TOTAL FITNESS

Located right here in beautiful Mananthavady, Total Fitness is so much more than just a place to lift weights. We believe in creating a space that feels like home—where everyone from beginners to pros feels comfortable, safe, and motivated.

Our clean, spacious facility is packed with modern equipment, but what really sets us apart is our people. Our friendly trainers take the time to know you, understand your goals, and guide you every step of the way. Step through our doors and let's build the best version of you, together.

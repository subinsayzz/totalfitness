ABOUT TOTAL FITNESS

Founded in Mananthavady, Total Fitness was built to provide Wayanad with a modern, motivating, and welcoming fitness facility for everyone, from beginners lifting their first dumbbell to experienced athletes.

Our training floor is fitted with genuine Star Trac resistance machines, dedicated free weight racks, and high-performance cardio decks. With certified personal trainers on the floor and private ladies batches, we give you the guidance and structure you need to hit your fitness goals.
